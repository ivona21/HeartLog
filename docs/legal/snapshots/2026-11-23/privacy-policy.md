HeartLog is operated by Ivona Malekovic Selimovic, an individual based at Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina. We are responsible for deciding why and how personal information is processed through HeartLog; data protection law calls this the data controller.

Contact us about privacy or your information at hello@heartlog.me

This policy covers heartlog.me and the HeartLog application.

### 2. Information we process

**Account information.** When you register, we process your email address, account identifier, and information needed to authenticate you and manage your sessions.

We use Supabase to provide email-and-password authentication, email confirmation and password recovery. Authentication information includes your email address, account identifiers and authentication tokens used to maintain your session.

**Emotion entries and journal notes.** We process the emotions you choose to save, any journal text you add, and associated timestamps and identifiers. Journal text is optional. The information you choose to include may reveal health information, religious beliefs, sexual orientation or other sensitive personal information.

**Technical information.** When you visit or use HeartLog, we and our service providers may process technical information needed to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the service. This can include IP addresses, request details and error logs. The information collected depends on the services and settings used to deliver HeartLog.

**Messages you send us.** If you contact us, we process your contact details, message and any information you choose to provide so that we can respond.

You do not need an account to explore the emotion wheel. Visitors may connect to the services that deliver HeartLog, which may process technical information as described above.

Your email address and the information needed for authentication are required to provide an account. You choose whether to save entries or add notes. Without the information needed for an account, we cannot provide the account-based features.
